## Devil’s Bargain

**Fiendish Patron**

Choose two titles for your Patron. These dictate their rules. Breaking them invokes your Patron’s wrath.

- *The Beast - *Never show mercy.
- *The Deceiver - *Deny the existence of the Deceiver.
- *The Fallen - *Always respect a holy site.
- *The Conqueror* - Never pass up an opportunity to show your superiority. Make followers or slaves of enemies.
- *The Accuser* - Be Meek and Deferential to Authority, conquer through betrayal.
- *The King of the Bottomless Pit* - All belongs to the king. Live frugally, pay and collect tribute.

The remaining four are the rivals to your Patron. You can earn your Patron’s favour by carrying out a deed that majorly acts against one of their rivals’ rules.

## Magisir

Wizard Survivor, Magic item collector, Addict.

**Curse Progression**

Tier 1: Graft a magic item to your body, or have it grafted by someone else. Requires ancient or experimental knowledge or a self-grafting item.

Tier 2: have at least 3 items grafted to your body

Tier 3: have at least 5 items grafted to your body

### Tier 1

The First magic item grafted to your body either gains a special ability or has its normal function improved. (see [https://www.bastionland.com/2015/12/wizzodds.html](https://www.bastionland.com/2015/12/wizzodds.html))

The gain of function is decided in conversation with the DM or by DM fiat.

**Self grafting**

You can now graft additional magic items onto your body yourself.

- The Grafting process takes d6 days.
- You take d10 STR damage(calculated on the last day)
- If the damage is enough to injure you, the injury you roll will decide how and where the item is grafted. Otherwise it is up to you.
- **Humanity**: you gain an additional stat called humanity, it starts equal to your Charisma or 10, whichever is higher. Each time you graft roll d4 and subtract it from your humanity.

**Humanity**

| Score | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 9 | Your body takes on an unnatural color. |
| 8 | Lower Charisma by 4 |
| 7-6 | You no longer need to eat but you are **deprived** unless you have consumed a charge in the last day. |
| 5-4 | Your body refuses the mundane, can no longer hold mundane items (including clothing) for more than a few seconds. |
| 3 | Any magic item you touch is automatically and painlessly grafted to your body |
| 0 | You lose your humanity and become a **Magigolum.** You have no needs except to obtain more items to graft to yourself |

### Tier 2

**Consume Charges**

You can consume charges of magic items (grafted or otherwise). Each charge restores d4 points in a stat of choice. Items that can be used once a day have 1 charge.

**Charge Items**

Your body can Generate magic item charges at a cost.

Trade Stat for charges, depending on item power, 2 stat points for a charge, 4 if its considered an extremely powerful item.

### Tier 3

Gain d4 free charges every rest, that you can divide amongst grafted items.
