# Ecclesiastic Jobs

A job granted by pledging yourself to (a) god or working in a religious institution.

**A character can only have one ecclesiastic job.** 

### Rituals

Ecclesiasts also have access to rituals. These can be performed without limit except for the requirements of the ritual itself.

## Priest of the One
Faith: The One God

> **About the One God:** There is One True God, also known as the Blind God, this is to be recognized by all. Only heretics and dim-witted people from the far country honour any other demon they call a ‘god’.
>
> Power from hierarchy.
>
> Other gods are the result of sins of sentient races. And must be driven out of our realm.

The Blind God is not some force in this world that gives you magic if you are nice. He grants power by allowing us to channel the holiness of those who came before us through relics.

### Tier 1: Acolyte

**Relic Mastery**: Though the power resides in the relics, only those like you that are trained by the church know the secret to using them. Spreading these secrets is a grave sin.

You are granted the use of Third-Class Relics. Churches must provide you with any relics you request, though it is considered bad form to take more than you need.

### Tier 2: Priest

You are granted the use of up 2 Second-Class Relics. You may borrow them from any church though not all churches have each type. The smallest churches only have one Second-Class Relic.

- Borrowing more than allowed may lead to excommunication or being hunted by church enforcers should it come to that.
- If additional relics are found unknown to the church you are to introduce them to the church. You may still use them in life threatening situations should they occur before then.

### Tier 3: Bishop

You are granted the use of up 3 Second-Class Relics and 2 First-Class Relics.

You get your own seat of power in your assigned diocese.

See the Relics page in the rulebook.

### Rituals

**Bless Water/ Oil**

Requires: One hour of chanting, hallowed ground

Create one dose (one waterskin) of holy water or oil.

**Life for a life**

A ‘willing’ servant of the one God can give his life in exchange for that of another. Requires the body of the dead(or at least the head and the heart) and that of the living.

- The ritual works only on those dead before their time, those that die from disease or old age are unaffected.
- Reviving those martyred in a heroic manner for their god are is seen as going against the will of god also.

This ritual is shrouded in secrecy and taboo. It is said that the most powerful in office pay regular insurance to a bishop in order to safeguard their life in case of assassination.

## Order of the Servant Returned
Faith: The One God

The servant of the one god is a mythical knight. He got pierced by 100 swords and was reborn 100 times in service to the One God. According to stories among those accepted into the other are those who help a servant complete a quest or recover a servant’s body or nail.

### Tier 1: servant

Followers are marked by having a golden nail inserted into their chest. The nail is used to magically track the body. Removing a nail is considered a grave sin.

Once a servant dies their corpse will rise again in three days. Once they return to a church the priest performs a boiling ritual to clean their bones.

As long as a servant does his prayers and keeps his gods favour he is free from the sickening miasma that accompanies undeath.

- They have the advantages and disadvantages of being a walking undead skeleton
  - Bladed(except heavy) weapons are impaired against you.
  - Injuries don’t heal normally, a priest of the one can restore you as well as any magic that might be able to mend bone
- Should they be destroyed, at least 51% of their bones can be taken to a church of The One God to be revived again.

## Screamer of Deiumos, Father of Monsters
Faith: The Powers

God of the monstrous, mutation and the unnatural order.

You have a sick love for monsters, but they don’t love you back.

### Tier 1: Acolyte

You learn the rituals of the monster.

### Tier 2: Monster-head chosen

Your head gets turned into that of a monster!

### Rituals

**Gathering**

Conditions

- Requires an immobile but living and awake human subject
- Requires a torch to be held by the caster and 5 large bonfires placed in a circle
- Requires 1 DT of chanting

Summon all local(or on local encounter table) monstrous entities. They will come forth and devour the subject, even if the monster does not usually consume humans. Unintelligent monsters will be charmed by your torch as long as it burns and can be given simple commands. Intelligent monsters will be prevented from attacking you but can only be ordered to keep a distance.

**Becoming**

Drip your own blood, drawn with a ritual dagger of Deiumos, into water while chanting to spread corruption.

- Affects a small still body of water like a well or a pond.

## Administrator of The Undead Bureaucracy
Faith: The Powers

The undead bureaucracy has installed itself as a layer before the afterlife. Recording any death they can and occasionally correcting a death or a life. The bureaucracy has many ranks, but even the highest ranked members are simply following rules set in place by the original founders.

Employees of the bureaucracy are respected across the land.

### Tier 1: Gravesinger and Collegiate registrar

Responsibilities: Sending the dead to their grave marking them as counted to protect them from undeath.

**Song of the dead**

Singing makes undead within earshot become lethargic, they will not move unless disturbed.

The singing can keep up singing for 1 DT at a time. If sung near a newly dead body it becomes protected from undeath.

**Marked for Death**

Through touching a target a Gravesinger can mark a target. Undead are attracted to it until the singer lifts the curse. The target will be attacked before others if possible. The target will be chased endlessly by undead that have taken notice of it.

### Tier 2: District secretary

Responsibilities: Bundle the deaths collected by the registrars. Investigate Notable deaths.

**Request forms** 

After a long rest, meditate to request 1+TIER forms from an undead court to get stamped for use.

Forms:

A31D - Call Revenant, Bring back a creature that died in the last day for 3d6 days.

X55J7 - Shorten Life, age target 3d6 years.

DD93 - Deputy Recruitment - make an undead creature serve you for 3d6 hours, intelligent undead will negotiate payment first and have the right to deny.

### Tier 3: Vice chancellor

Grant life or death. Only with a permit.

Summon spectral court of appeals.
