## Seraphic Crusader

Warriors from an obscure sect that seeks to spread the blind god’s word to the hells, to smite evil at what they believe to be it’s source.

They use magical armor that is melded into the wearers body transforms the wearer slowly. As they grow into their armor they ‘molt’ and are fitted with larger armor.

Only when the warrior grows into their third set of armor are they considered worthy of journeying to hell itself.

## Paladin

As a Paladin of the One God you must follow the following tenets

PROTECT THE INNOCENT

PROTECT THE FAITH

SMITE THE SINFUL

Should you seriously violate one of these tenets you fall, and become an anti-paladin, a tragic or malevolent figure.

**Anathema**

Certain powers have special effect on those that are Anathema to the faith.

- Evil Undead
- The Possessed
- Apostates.

### Tier 1: Initiate

**Charismata**

You are granted use of a holy amulet that incorporates part of a grave stone of a warrior saint. When meditating on the symbol during a long rest a paladin can prepare Charismata to use during the day.

- You can prepare Paladin Tier +1 charismata each day.
- You can prepare the same blessing multiple times.

### Tier 2: Paladin

As a full member you gain the right of **intercession.** You can halt any judgement meted out by church or law(in faithful lands) until reviewed by a senior member of the clergy.

You also have the right of **aid**, meaning that a church or holy community must at the very least:

- offer free religious services to you and your companions
- grant access and use of any armaments for yourself only
- offer the temporary help of minor servants as long as you keep them from harm

At the same time the church has more rights over you as well:

- You cannot deny a request for aid from a church if you are able to assist
- You cannot accept payment for such a request (your companions can but in negotiations you take the side of the church)

### Tier 3: Paladin Lord (lvl 5)

You can initiate others into the order.

Gain one unique paladin blessing. (create yourself or pick from examples)

**Examples:**

**Sacrifice the Strong:** Able to Resurrect someone who died within the last day by taking a permanent injury, only once.

**Palalichdom:** The good is not yet finished with you. The next time you would die you are reborn as an undead-holy warrior(fully healed, except for major injuries) . You suffer the benefits and burdens of being undead.

**A Good Life:** Your natural time of death delayed by 100 years and you aren’t effected by old age.

**Loved by Nature**: All natural creatures are friendly to you.

**Battlefield Luck:** You will never be struck by ranged/siege weapons/projectile spells fired at long distance.

You gain a Paladin Lord Trait:

**d8 Paladin Lord Traits**

1. Butterflies and songbirds are sitting on and traveling with you
2. A shimmering halo can be seen around your head when in combat
3. All hair except that on your head is replaced by soft feathers
4. You are untouched by rain and snow
5. Body parts and blood shed from your body does not decay.
6. Your blood can cure non-magical poisons
7. Any song describing your deeds sounds good.
8. Anathema to your order cannot pronounce your name.

### Paladin Charismata

**Turn Unholy**

Speed: Quick

Up to d4+tier Undead or creatures Anathema to your Order must make a MND save or be turned. Unintelligent targets flee mindlessly and may get stuck in a corner. Intelligent targets avert their eyes and make a morale check, if they succeed they still take an additional d6 dmg from attacks in the same round.

**Smite**

An attack charged with holy fervour that does a bonus d10 melee damage, 2d10 vs undead/ anathema.

**Summon Steed**

Call forth a (natural) steed to serve you! In a city a horse or hog might serve you (its owner might be dismayed), in the wild a different type of horse or hog.

- The steed will serve you until a fitting moment comes for it to leave (after you reach your destination or complete a quest).
- If you love your steed dearly you can attempt to convince it to stay (similar to hiring a retainer).
- You have advantage to Tests against being dismounted from this steed.

**Healing Hands**

Restore d4 stat points or cure a minor injury or disease.

**Holy Shield**

Deflect as many spells as your tier during 1 round or any projectile with your shield(which must have a holy symbol engraved or painted on it). Can be used to protect nearby allies.

**Interrogate**

Demand a straight answer(something that can be shouted in one breath). MND save to resist, undead and anathema to your order have disadvantage.

**Imposed Duel**

Use holy imposition to force an amount of foes up to your tier to duel you. MND save to resist.

While duelling you can only target each other.

**Absorb Curse**

Take a curse or corruption from someone else upon yourself.

## Anti-Paladin

A fallen Paladin.

**Falling**

When you fall as a paladin you gain as many tiers as you had in the paladin job. Anti-paladins often start as tragic figures

### Tier 1: Exiled Initiate

**Curses**

When meditating during a long rest an anti-paladin can prepare curses.

- You can prepare anti-paladin Tier +1 cursus each day.
- You can prepare the same curse multiple times.

### Tier 2: Anti-Paladin

Roll for d8 one Anti-Paladin Trait

**Anti-Paladin Traits:**

1. Tears continuously stream from your eyes
2. Plants wither at your touch(d6 for unarmed attacks vs plants)
3. Skin is blackened as if by coal that does not wash off
4. Your hair(including bodyhair) goes pure white, if it was already pure white it goes bright red.
5. Light doesn’t illuminate your face fully, it is always wreathed in shadow.
6. Armor you are wearing painfully grows into your skin and takes up no more slots.
7. You cast no shadow.
8. The air around you is freezing, it pains you but you are otherwise unaffected by cold weather.

**Unholy**

You are *exposed* on ground holy to the One God, can be repelled by turn unholy used by paladins.

### Tier 3 (lvl5): Light-eater

Pick one Light-eater Ability:

- Gravity rejects you, you can levitate at will, with a maximum speed of 3km/h.
- You can see in perfect darkness, your eyes glow a dim red when you do so.
- After two rounds of building up speed you can run as fast as a horse while going in one direction.
- You can sprout an animated 3 meter chain[d8, long] under your control from each hand.

### Anti-Paladin Curses:

**~~Turn Unholy~~ Turn Holy**

d4+tier Creatures Holy to your Order and those belonging to it must make a MND save (at disadvantage) or be turned. Unintelligent targets flee mindlessly and may get stuck in a corner. Intelligent targets avert their eyes and make a morale check, if they succeed they still take an additional d6 dmg from attacks that round.

**~~Smite~~ Dark Healing:**

Restore d4+tier stat points or cure one injury in one person by inflicting it on another you touch.

**~~Summon Steed~~ Steeding**

Call forth a creature (or human) with less MND than you and break its mind for 24 hours, Turning it into a servant. Its eyes will turn white.

You have advantage to checks against being dismounted from your servant.

**~~Healing Hands~~ Sap Life**

Your healing hand has gone bad. Touch someone to take siphon d4+tier stat points(for 1 DT) or cause a minor injury.

**~~Holy Shield~~ Unholy Weapon:**

Any weapon you wield is coated by the poison of your sin. It can poison water on touch, causing those who drink it to throw up violently. And deals d6 bonus damage if it draws blood.

**~~Interrogate~~ Torturous Touch**

Your touch brings immense pain if you will it.

Demand one straight answer(something that can be shouted in a breath). STR save, those holy to your original order have advantage. No save for the weak (i.e. peasants and such).

**~~Imposed Duel~~ Deny Mercy**

For 1 DT, d4+tier cowardly or weak foes that can see you have impaired attacks against you and cannot flee from you.

**~~Absorb Curse~~ Cursed Sword**

Absorb a curse from a creature into a bladed weapon. It transfers to the next creature it penetrates.

**Dark Magic**

Learn a tier 1 spell from a spellbook you have access to, which you can cast as if using a blessing. The spell is forgotten when a different blessing is prepared.

## Berserker

### Tier 1: Berserker

You can unleash the beast inside you. You can go into a rage once per each mastered Tier of berserker:

**Rage**

- +d6 dmg to attacks you make
- **Cleaving**: if you kill something during your rage you (must) make another attack, without limit.
- The rage ends if you don’t attack or get attacked for 2 rounds in a row
- When your rage ends you pass out for 1DT

**Involuntary rage**

Each time you take STR damage you must make a MND test or go into a rage.

- This counts against your daily rage uses

### Tier 2: Veteran Berserker

**Raging Strength**

Advantage to strength tests while raging.

**Power Attack**

Add d8 damage to an attack with any (improvised) weapon, destroying the weapon in the process.

- If you attempt this with a weapon that is indestructible through magic you take d8 damage yourself as well.

### Tier 3A (lvl5): Bestial Warrior

Unbridled Rage:

- Gain 6 Guard at the start of your rage that can exceed your max Gd
- Your melee attacks deal +d8 and deal AoE damage (reach AoE for long weapons).
- You must attack a creature each turn if you can and your rage only stops when all your (perceived) enemies are slain or you are knocked unconscious

### Tier 3B (lvl5): Master Berserker

Controlled Rage:

- You can enter and exit rage at will without passing out
- You will not enter a rage involuntarily
- **Cleaving** is no longer forced.

### Spellblade Abilities

Each ability costs 1 MP to use.

**Living Weapon**

Can enchant a Melee weapon to fight independently for [sum] turns, while within mid. range of the caster. It floats and attacks as if you are wielding it.

**Spellcutter(bladed only)**

You can cut through (spell) projectiles(destroying them) or blast spells (protecting yourself and a wedge directly behind you). Also works vs abilities like fire breath.

**Grow**

Your weapon grows three times its size. gain long reach, and does d12 blast dmg for [sum] rounds.

**Thunder Bolt** **(polearm only)**

Throw a polearm into the air, lightning strikes where it lands at the end of the round, doing [sum] blast damage.

**Floating shield**

Enchant a shield to float around you for 1+[dice] DT. The shield still grants you 1 armor and stops all basic projectiles.

**Chain-growth(small weapons only)**

Your weapon grows an ethereal chain and handle for an hour, granting you long reach. Swings can target two creatures within mid. range.

**Throwing**

Can throw your weapon accurately up to very long range. It returns at the start of your next turn.

**Elemental Blade**

Your weapon does (fire, cold, lightning) type damage instead of physical for 1+[sum] DT. Weapon also emits this element passively without harming you.

**Ghost Blade**

The weapon ignores armor for 1+[sum] rounds.

### Master Spellblade Abilities

**All swords day**

## Ranger
Status: work in progress

They patrol the wilds to protect civilization.

(archery covered in different job. herbalism included since nobody would want that job :) )

You can’t make use of your ranger abilities while wearing heavy armor.

### Tier 1: Woodsman

#### Survival

- Always finds +1 ration when attempting to hunt.
- Advantage to any save related to tracking

#### Basic Herbalism

You are skilled at finding ad-hoc medical supplies in nature.

- Looking for herbs takes one DT
- You can find herbs once for free when setting up camp in the wilderness
- After harvesting, herbs remain potent for 24 hours

**Herbalism and environment**

Consider the environment before rolling. A completely alien environment or one thats completely sterile should not allow a roll at all, and a bare place like the desert should give disadvantage. Only give advantage in terrains the ranger knows like the back of their hand.

**You can attempt to find the following types of herb:**

1. Minor Curative, restores 1 STR
2. Salving herb, restores 2 STR once for burn wounds
3. Antihemorrhagic, restores 2 STR once to those who suffered a recent injury
4. Minor Poison, deals d6 STR damage when consumed
5. Paralitic, deals d6 DEX damage when consumed
6. Medicant, halts the effect of a poison for an hour
7. Minor Sedative, makes sleepy when consumed
8. Warming herb, stops you from being deprived due to cold

| Roll relative Mind | Do you find the Herb |
| --- | --- |
| +5 | No, and… you disturb the wilds, roll for an encounter |
| +1-4 | No, but… you find an herb that looks about right, 2 in 6 chance it has the desired effect |
| 0-4 | You find the Herb you want |
| -1-4 | Yes, you find 2 doses |
| -5 | Yes, you find a trove of 2+d4 doses |
