# Magic User Jobs

In the world there exist many forms of arcane knowledge, these are called Tomes. They may once have been created by a great wizard, a demon or a god. Nowadays they are used by magic users to master the spells within.

#### Tiers

Spells in a tome are learned in TIERS, in the same way other jobs grant abilities per tier.

- Tier 1 spells cost 1mp to cast, 2 cost MP and so on.
- Third tiers require an additional source of knowledge related to the tome. Such as the original writer’s notes.

#### Unmastered Tomes

Just owning a tome grants some benefits.

- You can perform any rituals in the tome
- You can cast the first spell listed

> Not all tomes are books, some may be burdensome items and some might be restricted to one area

### Spells: types and descriptions

### Tricks

Besides spells, some tomes also teach you a trick. Tricks are small magical effects you can summon at no cost.

### Spell descriptions

**Range**

-  Spells range is expressed through range bands see
- Touch, touch spells either freely made against disabled or grappled targets or are part of a melee attack.
  - **Touch attack** d4 dmg, only affects Guard. If the target has no guard left, no STR damage is taken from the touch but the spell succeeds.

**AoE**

- Expressed through range bands, emanating from the chosen point within casting range.

**Speed** Speed determines in what phase in combat a spell will be cast. You always prepare to cast a spell in the preparation phase.

- **Base**, when speed isn’t mentioned in a description, use the base speed. Spells are cast during the action phase.
- **Quick**, spells can be cast during the quick phase without moving, or in the action phase when combined with movement.
- **Slow**, these spells are cast during the release phase.

**Reverse Casting**

Some spells have clear and known opposite versions that are listed in the description. For other spells a magic user can attempt to reverse the spell.

- Make a MND test, if you succeed you cast a reversed version of the spell
- Should you fail, roll the related corruption die
- The details of the reversed spell will be specified by de DM

### Rituals

Rituals are large scale spells with severe limitations on casting. For example some rituals may only be cast with the help of a group of other people, or only castable once a lifetime.

- All rituals noted in the tome are available after mastering the first tier.

**Overcast**

Some spells can be made more powerful by spending more MP

**Concentration:** Some spells require concentration. This means that while the magic user is keeping the spell active he cannot do anything else other than move up to mid. range.

**Additional Cost**

Some spells have an additional cost. These might be components(used up by the casting) a catalyst(not used up) or a corruption roll.

**Default Values**

Spells don’t always have all their descriptors. In this case it’s usually obvious. A bolt of lightning has no duration and a spell that gives you a giant nose obviously has a range of self.

**Light(reversible)**

> Reversible spells are kind of like two spells.

Range: mid

AoE: mid

Duration: 6 rounds/lvl (half for darkness)

The caster uses his finger to trace an ancient symbol of light in the air. A globe of light is created. The spell may be cast upon a location in the air, upon a physical object (in which case it will move with the object), or upon a creature (in which case a DEX test is applicable). If the creature succeeds the saving throw, the globe will center upon the space just behind the intended victim. The caster may extinguish the magical light at any time.

If targeting a creatures eyes it has advantage on the Dex test if it fails its blinded for the duration.

The reverse of the spell, darkness, creates an area of utter darkness just as the light spell creates light, with the only distinction being that the darkness spell has half the duration of a light spell.

**Silence**

Range: mid

AoE: reach

Duration: 2 rounds/lvl

The caster uses his finger to trace an ancient symbol of silence in the air. This brings into being a sphere of complete and utter silence in which no sound can be heard. The spell may be cast upon a location in the air, upon a physical object (in which case it will move with the object), or upon a creature (in which case a DEX test is applicable). If the creature makes a saving throw, the area of silence will centre upon the space just behind the intended victim.

The caster can end the silence at any time.

TIER 2

> TIER two spells are available after mastering the second tier, meaning you study the tome twice.

**Sleep**

Range: mid

AoE: mid

Duration: 5 rounds/lvl

The caster summons shimmering purple light to illuminate an area. Only creatures that can see are affected.

- Small and medium-sized Creatures below 10 STR immediately fall into a magical sleep
- those with 10-12 can make a STR test.
- Larger or stronger creatures will be made docile and drowsy if they are not currently in an alerted state.

Magically sleeping creatures may be killed or tied up at a rate of one per round by a single person.

A sleeping creature requires a full round to waken and must be shaken or slapped to bring it to consciousness; mere noise, however loud, will not disturb the enchanted slumber of a sleep spell’s victim.

**The steps of Twilight**

Fall asleep. In your dream you will find a stairway made of light, you can climb it to enter the dreams of someone you know, who is sleeping.

**Haste**

Range: mid

Duration: 3 rounds + lvl

Affects: 1 creature/ level

This spell imbues creatures in the targeted area with preternatural speed. This has effects on combat:

- Affected creatures can attack twice, still taking the only the highest result as normal

And spellcasting:

- Wizards can theoretically cast two spells a round

  - Make a corruption test for any TIER 2 spell
  - TIER 3 spells cannot be cast in the same round as another spell

The spell ages the creatures upon which it is cast by 2 years(see old age condition).

- The occupants of the areas also cannot be directly targeted by attacks or spells. Though they are not immune to things like gasses.

**Magic Jar**

The caster’s body enters a comatose trance as their life-force is transferred into a receptacle (any hollow inanimate object within mid. range), known as a *magic jar*. From there, the caster may attempt to possess the bodies of other creatures.

**When the caster’s life-force is in the magic jar:**

- **Possession:** The caster may attempt to possess the body of another creature within long range. The victim can pass a MND test to resist possession. If the test is successful, the caster may not make another attempt to possess that victim for one turn. If the test fails, the caster’s life-force leaves the magic jar and enters the victim, possessing it.
- **Returning:** The caster may choose to return to their own body at any time, ending the spell.
- **If the magic jar is destroyed:** The caster dies.
- **If the caster’s own body is destroyed:** Their life-force is stranded in the magic jar.

**When the caster’s life-force is possessing a victim:**

- **Control:** The caster gains full control over the victim’s body. Its hard to control the body, lose d6 Dexternity.
- **If the magic jar is destroyed:** The spell ends and the caster’s life-force is trapped in the possessed body.
- **If the possessed victim is killed:** The caster’s life-force returns to the magic jar.
- **Dispelling:** [***Dispel evil***](https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Dispel_Evil) forces the caster’s life-force back into the magic jar.
- **If the caster’s own body is destroyed:** Their life-force is stranded in the body of the creature being possessed.

**Teleportation**

Target Range: mid

Teleportation Range: anywhere on plane

The caster or a chosen creature vanishes and reappears at a location of the caster’s choosing.

- **Gear:** The subject is teleported with all its gear, up to its maximum load.
- **Unwilling subjects:** May attempt a MND test to prevent the teleportation.
- **Destination:** May be at any distance, but must be known to the caster. The destination must be an open space at ground level. (It is not possible to intentionally teleport the subject into mid-air or into solid matter.)
- **Risk:** There is a risk, when teleporting, of accidentally arriving above or below ground level. The chance of a successful teleportation depends on the caster’s knowledge of the destination (see below). Roll d% and consult the table below.

| Knowledge of Dest. | Ground Level | Too High | Too Low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scant | 01–50 | 51–75 | 76–00 |
| Moderate | 01–80 | 81–90 | 91–00 |
| Exact | 01–95 | 96–99 | 00 |

**Ground level:** The subject appears at the intended destination.

**Too high:** The subject appears 2d6 meters above the intended destination. If this causes the subject to appear inside solid matter, they die instantly. Otherwise, the subject falls from a height.

**Too low:** The subject appears below the surface of the ground and dies instantly.

**Knowledge of destination:** The caster’s knowledge of the destination is rated as follows:

- **Scant:** A location that the caster has visited once or twice, has seen by magical scrying, or has heard of from descriptions.
- **Moderate:** A location that the caster has visited often or has studied via scrying for several weeks.
- **Exact:** A location that the caster has made a detailed study of, in person.
