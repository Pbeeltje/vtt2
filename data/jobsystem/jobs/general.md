## Alchemist

A maker of potions.

As described in: [Naively Simple Alchemy](https://foreignplanets.blogspot.com/2020/07/naively-simple-alchemy.html)

### Potions

All potions require four things:

1. Oil, provides modifier (adjective)
2. Powder, provides noun/verb
3. Catalyst, provides potency and flavour
4. Alchemical supplies (1 slot)

#### Oils and Powders

All things can be reduced to either **Oils** or **Powders** by use of **Alchemical Equipment**. Creatures generally provide only 1 oil or 1 powder, unless they’re very large.

- These substances produce a single, unchanging effect based on what they were derived from.
- **Oils** provide an adjective effect and **Powders** denote a particular verb or noun.
- An alchemist can distil up to three oils and or potions in one DT.

When new oils and powders are discovered the Alchemist should note them down in their journal.

#### Creating a potion

A single Oil and a single Powder are combined to create a potion, the effects are the combination of the Adjective and Verb/Noun. This is combined with one catalyst which lends the potion additional flavour as well as determining its potency

- A potion takes two periods to brew
- 1 Oil and 1 Powder are used up per potion
- Catalyst and alchemical supplies are not used up

A created potion is named as follows:

*Potion of Adjective Noun/ Verb (Catalyst)*

Example:

Potion of Growing Earth (*Herod’s Horn*),
maybe this potion can grow small earthen objects into large ones like a sand castle. Due to the potency of the catalyst, the earthen object increases ten times in size.

**Extended Example**

an amateur adventuring alchemist slays two *Blink-Dogs* and reduces one into an **Oil** and the other into a **Powder** using his alchemical equipment. Using his **Alchemical Equipment**, he is aware of the effect the *Blink-Dog* **Oil** and *Blink-Dog* **Powder** will bring to any particular potion. (These effects are improv’d by the DM)

*Blink-Dog* **Oil** = Capricious

*Blink-Dog* **Powder** = Teleport

Having discovered these effects, he (and the DM) log the effects into a codified alchemical grimoire for future reference. Before the Alchemist can make his Capricious Teleport potion (drinking this potion will cause random teleportation), he must select a **Catalyst**. **Catalysts** are rare and collectable magical artifacts that allow and modify the synthesis of potions. **Catalysts** dictate the Tone and Potency (and flavour) of the Potion. (the effect of **Catalysts** are known only to the DM)

This Alchemist only has one **Catalyst**, *A Hand of Glory*, a **Catalyst** of middling potency that makes potions Gothic. It takes 4 hours to distil a potion, but when complete the potion is recorded as 'Potion of Capricious Teleport (*Hand of Glory*)’. The **Catalyst** used, means that this Potion will last for 10 minutes and each teleport will be accompanied by a cloud of black, sulphurous vapour and an almost silent screaming sound.

### Tier 1

Can create potions and distil powders and oils.

**Alchemical Base**

You can create an alchemical base potion using human blood (1 STR cost) in place of oil and powder.

The alchemical base can be combined with an oil and powder to instantly create a potion with one step lower potency.

### Tier 2

- Distilling powders or oils yields d4 results instead of only 1.
- You can mix two oils and one powder, this has a 2/6 chance of failure

Failures:

1. **Impotence**, you brew a useless fluid
2. **Corruption**, breathing the fumes and handling the product changes you, gain the next step in a random magic corruption table.
3. **Explosion**, 2d10 blast damage, medium range. Supplies destroyed.
4. **Unstable Reaction**, all three ingredients become something else, the alchemists knows its wrong but not what it does.
5. **Immediacy**, you or the area (depending on the potion) is immediately affected by the potion.
6. **Alchemists bane**, you create a ruinous poison. If disposed in the wild it slowly kills all wildlife around it. If ingested causes a wasting disease that decreases all stats by 1 a day. The eventual corpse not rot.

### Catalysts

Catalysts are unique, usually named, items. They consist of a potency and a flavour.

Potency of a potion is determined by potency of the catalyst. Sometimes this is further modified by the quality of the ingredients, particularly mundane or rare ingredients might increate or decrease the potency by one step.

Guide for effects of potency:

| Potency | Duration | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Weak* | 1-10 rounds | 1d4 |
| *Middling* | 1 DT | 1d8 |
| *Strong* | a day to a month | 2d10 |
| *Extreme* | a month to permanent | 2d20 |

Flavour is an additional modifier of the potion. A potion of Rapid Healing might have the effect of healing an injury or restoring STR. If it was brewed with the crown of the fire-devil the effect could manifest as a line of flame that passes over their body like fire burning a sheet of paper.

### Alchemical Supplies

The most basic supplies include a small mortar and pestle, some beakers and small cutting tools.

More advanced supplies, like an entire alchemy lab, would allow an alchemist to brew many potions at once and grant a chance at increased potency (2 in 6).

## Monster Tamer

### Tier 1: Monster Fan

#### Monster Training

Monsters under your control can gain levels through training, see training levels.

Training a monster is a downtime activity:

Roll under, base chance of success is 1/20

- Training equipment appropriate for monster +6
- (live?)Food monster likes +4
- Training in appropriate biome +5
- Assistant trainer +3

#### Training Levels

Base: 7 morale, it attacks you upon failure.
Riding the monster requires passing a dexterity save.

lvl 1: 8 morale, it hides upon morale failure.
Riding the creature doesn’t require a save, if its sufficiently large enough to ride relative to the rider.

lvl 2: 9 morale, it cowers but stays in place upon morale failure.
You can send it to perform a task out of sight and it will return if it can.

lvl 3: The monster gains 4 Guard

### Tier 2: Monster Tamer

You have advantage on saves related to taming and training.

Your deeper understanding of monsters allows you to communicate more complex orders to them at about the level of a well trained dog (fetch, play dead, hide etc.).

### Tier 3 (lvl 5): Monster Lord

- There’s no limit to the amount of monsters you can control.
- Spending this much time raising monsters you grow strange:

  **Roll d6 for a Monster Lord Trait**

  1. You feel monsters emotions through skin contact but you can no longer stand to wear armor.
  2. You only drink homemade monster milk.
  3. You are sexually attracted to monsters.
  4. You move on all fours
  5. You never wash or remove any body hair
  6. You become a follower of Deiumos, Father of monsters, your next job point must be spent on ‘Acolyte of Deiumos’.
- Also Pick a skill:

  **Monster Engineer**, you are skilled at making monster prosthetics and refitting armor to suit monsters.

  **Monster Scientist**, for a cost of 50gp worth of reagents, or random things you find, you can experiment on a monster you control. This process takes a full day.
  The monster rolls for a mutation. You get one reroll per try.

  **Monster Knight**, you specialize in riding monsters,

  **Monster Fuser,** fuse your body into that of one of your monsters in a one month process. You can choose your form. You keep your MND and CHA but take other stats from the monster.

  1. Lower body fused into back of monster.
  2. Human head on monster body.
  3. Back fused into monster stomach.

## Monster Connoisseur

Monster chef/ vivisectionist.

### Tier 1

**Extract**

Extract parts from a monster without destroying them.

**Cook**

Make a meal out of parts of one monster.

Each monster has one or more(for rare monsters) parts that grant special effects when cooked according to the connoisseur tradition.

These meals are unstable and must be consumed in 8 hours or they lose their effect.

Parts have an effect that is expressed in a phrase with three parts:

## Knower of Words (or Friend of Spirits)

One who has mastered using a true name of a concept that makes up reality.

**Questing:**

To parse a word out of our reality you have to stand outside of it. It takes 1d30 days spent in another dimension to discover your word. Some are also born knowing a word.

> The mind is only capable of ever grasping one word.

**Using a word**

Once you know a word you can think of any type of magic directly related to the word and attempt to cast it. (basic word magic is 2d6+lvl vs target)

**With a few caveats:**

- As the task becomes more difficult you have to hit a higher target with your dice roll.
- You need some form of the word as a catalyst or fuel for the spell.
- If you fail to cast a certain form of the spell you can’t attempt that same form again for 24 hours.

**Damage:**

Basic word incantations will do between d4 and d8 damage. In certain situations you may recieve a bonus roll between d4 and d12 similar to regular attacks. For incantations affecting multiple targets divide the damage between the targets equally or, for a more difficult target # to divide it at your choosing.

See word magic document with some changes below:

**Miscast:**

If your word was ‘fire’ for example, fire will not warm you, cook your food or light your way.

If you roll a 2 on a spell roll. roll a d6, on a 1 the word turns against you for a number of days equal to the challenge number you were trying to beat with your roll.

If it was ‘birds’ random birds might attack you.

### Twinjob Friend of Spirits:

Same rules as above except you befriend a natural spirit.

**Questing:** Make contact with a spirit and pledge your soul to further the well being of said spirit. The spirit of water may be contacted by drowning in a holy river and so on.

## Blue Mage

Blue magic calls upon natural energies more primal than magic. The user can gain access to abilities reserved for monsters by consuming them. As they advance in their studies, blue mages gradually become more adept at using these stolen techniques themselves.

### Learning Abilities

Abilities learned by eating the heart of a creature.

If you know as many abilities as you can store you pick one to lose.

EATING MONSTERS IS DANGEROUS. use monstermenu pdf.

> Eating a wizards heart gives you random spell he knows (cast as though wizard lvl = blue mage tier).

### Using Abilities

You can use the abilities at will, may require free movement depending on the ability(a gag would stop dragons breath for example).

Cost: STR damage to cast, with no injury rolls

For passive abilities the cost will be deducted from your STR attribute until you unlearn the ability.

### Known Abilities

Dragon’s Breath
Petrifying Gaze
Phase(spider)
Eye-beam

(two ways to make an ability: come up with something unique each time, or literally copy a trait of a creature, second i think is easier but many monsters will not really have an ability)

### Tier 1

2 slots to store copied abilities.

### Tier 2

3 slots to store copied abilities.

Can speak to any creature you currently know an ability of.

### Tier 3

4 slots to store copied abilities.

Pick one:

1. Perfect body: Passive abilities have no cost
2. Tyrant: Enemies who’s abilities you have must make a morale test to attack you. As an action you can attempt to command them, MND test to resist.

## Healer

Trained in first aid and minor surgery.

> Medical supplies, 1/2 slot.

### Healing Injuries

**Major Injury**

By using up medical supplies you have a 2-6 chance of mending a permanent injury from mundane causes. Must happen during battle or directly after. One attempt per injury.

**Minor Injury**

By using up medical supplies you have a 4-6 chance of mending a temporary injury, one attempt allowed per injury.

### Restoring Stats

Can use medical supplies during long rest to restore 1d4+1 stat points to a character.

## Acrobat

Requirements: Minimum Dexternity of 14.

> **No Acrobat bonusses or abilities apply when encumbered or wearing heavy armor!**

### Tier 1: Tumbler

- +4 Guard
- No fall damage for falls of up to 15 feet.
- Climbing +4
- Running Jump of 12 feet.

### Tier 2: Acrobat

- +2 additional Guard
- No fall damage for falls of up to 30 feet.
- Can attempt to climb slick surfaces at -4.

**Great Escape**

Once per day, you can escape from something that is restraining you and that you could plausibly escape from. This includes grapples, lynchings, and awkward social situations, but not sealed coffins.

### Tier 3: Aerialist

- +2 additional Guard
- Running Jump distance of 20 feet. If there is a stable wall, you can instead wall run 30ft.

**Uncanny Dodge**

Whenever you make a DEX save for half damage, receive no damage on success and half on fail.
